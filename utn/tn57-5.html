Unicode Technical Note #57

Encoding and Shaping of the Mongolian Script

Version 5

Summary

This document provides a system of text representation and text shaping rules for the Mongolian script, covering the Hudum, Todo, Sibe, and Manchu writing systems and their Ali Gali extensions. It fixes the characters and the written units of each writing system, gives the step-by-step shaping rules that turn a sequence of characters into written units, and describes the architecture behind them, their history, the relationship to the Unicode Standard, and the data and toolchain that maintain the specification.

Modifications

The following summarizes modifications from the previous version of this document.

5

  • Rewrite the document throughout and reorganize it into chapters on the architecture, the writing systems, non-joining characters, and single-font implementation, with appendices on the background, phonology, the Unicode Standard, and comparison.
  • Add the history of the encoding and a phonemic analysis of the written languages.
  • Rename the Ali Gali letters ·H, ·M, ·T to ·X, together with several letters and written units of the writing systems.
  • Revise the data of every writing system: remove unattested forms, correct the variants and their FVS assignments, renumber variants, and update the shaping rules, letter classes, and mapping types accordingly.
  • Add particle mapping tables for Hudum, Todo, Sibe, and Manchu, and add tables of the written units and characters shared by the writing systems.
  • Align the document with the Unicode Standard documents that cite this UTN, and with the Script Encoding Working Group’s guidance at UTC #189.
  • Publish the document as a website rather than as a PDF.

The Modifications chapter of the document lists the changes in detail.
