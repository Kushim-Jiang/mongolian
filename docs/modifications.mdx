The following summarizes modifications from the previous versions of this document.

## Version 5

- Polish the wording throughout.
- Add an introduction to the history of the encoding and a phonemic analysis of the written languages.
- Rename the Ali Gali letters _·H_, _·M_, _·T_ to _·X_.
- Remove the transliterations of single-letter written units and phonetic letters. Add _bowed written unit_ to all letter class tables. Rename “Lookups” to “Mapping type” in all Mongolian-specific phase tables. Update the onset and devsger logic for _n_, _t_, _d_; rename `devsger` in “4. Devsger” to `vowel_devsger`; and split `post_bowed`/`post_bowed_feminine` and `feminine`/`feminine_marked` to trigger distinct shaping rules per consonant class. Add particle mapping tables for Hudum, Todo, Sibe and Manchu.
- Align the document with the Unicode Standard documents that cite this UTN and with what their citations ask of it.
- Align the document with the Script Encoding Working Group’s guidance on language-dependent glyph selection, given at UTC #189.
- Reorganize the chapters: move the introduction’s structural overview into Architecture, give the phoneme inventory an appendix of its own, and make the toolchain a section of the introduction.
- Publish the document as a website rather than as a PDF, with the Contents of the UTN page linking to it.
- Add a chapter on non-joining characters—the other letters, digits, and punctuation.
- Add a chapter on single-font implementation, gathering the written units and the characters of the supported writing systems into one table each.
- For Hudum,
  - Remove _Zr.medi_; the word _m o zr i k2 a n_ becomes _m o hh i k2 a n_.
  - Note _W_ in letters _ee_ and _w_.
  - Remove the EAC note: shaping is now required by GB 25914—2023 alone.
  - Remove `vowel_devsger` for _u_: a _u_ that a vowel precedes is written with the feminine form of its position, which `feminine` selects as it does after a consonant, so that the gender a word’s first vowel fixes carries to the vowel that ends the word.
  - Remove `devsger` for initial _n_: the initial _n_ that a consonant follows is written _N_ rather than _A_.
- For Todo,
  - Merge the written unit _I3_ into _Ip_: move _I.fina_ to _Ip.fina_, likewise the final ligated variant of _Ip_. The fabricated _Ip.fina_ and _i.fina.1_ are removed (contextual in the ligature).
  - Remove the unattested _N.fina_, _Gp.fina_, _oe.medi.1_, _oe.fina.1_, the written unit _Y_, _O.medi_ and _U.fina_.
  - Add _Zz2_ (of _zr_), as required by the Chinese national standard.
  - Assign FVS2 to medial _N2_ of _n_ and add _n.isol.2_; update the related note.
  - Remove the note on _ny_ and its reference: the form is attested in Todo literature only inside a Sanskrit word.
  - Treat _lvs_ as a vowel.
  - Add the missing “2. Syllabic”.
- For Sibe,
  - Add _Ni.medi_ (in _e_).
  - Use _M3_ for _M.fina_.
  - Remove the unattested _G.fina_, written unit _Gx_ (of _k_), _Ch.fina_, _Cc.fina_ and _W.fina_ (of _w_).
  - Remove _Zs.fina_, analyzed as _Zs2.medi_ followed by _I.fina_; add _Zs2_ (of _z_), moving the initial and medial ligated variants of _Zs_ to _Zs2_; assign FVS1 to _Zs2_ and FVS2 to _Zs_ in _z_; update the related note. The Sibe _Zs_ + _I_ ligature has no Manchu counterpart, so it is a distinct written unit rather than a system-specific ligature judgment.
  - Renumber _i.medi.2_ as _i.medi.1_ and _i.medi.3_ as _i.medi.2_; move _i.fina.3_ to _i.fina.0_; delete _i.fina.1_ (contextual in the ligature) and _i.fina.2_ (analyzed as _sbm.medi.0_ followed by _i.fina.0_); remove the related note.
  - Swap _t.isol.0_ with _t.isol.1_, _t.init.1_ with _t.init.2_, and _t.medi.1_ with _t.medi.3_.
  - Move the `marked` mapping of _z_ to _i_; update the onset and devsger logic for _k_, _g_, _h_.
- For Manchu,
  - Add _Ni.medi_ (in _e_).
  - Use _Hx2_ (of _k_) for _Hx.fina_.
  - Use _M3_ for _M.fina_.
  - Remove _Zs.fina_, analyzed as _Zs.medi_ followed by _Iy.fina_; remove the unattested _Ch.fina_, _Cc.fina_, _J.fina_, _Jc.fina_ and _W.fina_.
  - Renumber _i.medi.2_ as _i.medi.1_ and add _i.medi.2_; renumber _i.fina.2_ as _i.fina.1_ and _i.fina.3_ as _i.fina.2_; remove _i.fina.1_ (contextual in the ligature) and _i.fina.4_ (analyzed as _sbm.medi.0_ followed by _i.fina.2_); remove the related note.
  - Swap _t.isol.0_ with _t.isol.1_, _t.init.1_ with _t.init.2_, and _t.medi.1_ with _t.medi.3_.
- For Hudum Ali Gali,
  - Move _A2.fina_ into the final ligated variant of _A_ (contextual in the ligature); remove the phonetic letter _aX_ and the related note.
  - Remove the unattested _Pg.fina_, _Rh.fina_ and _Wp.init_.
  - Rename the letter _h_ to _hh_.
  - Move _hX.medi.1_ to _hh.medi.0_.
  - Remove the mapping type `post_wa` (part of the ligature).
- For Todo Ali Gali,
  - Remove _Hp.init_, which is actually _N.init_.
  - Rename the written unit _Zc_ to _Zz2_ and the letter _jX_ to _zr_.
  - Rename the letter _phX_ to _p_.
  - Remove _Ip.fina_ and _i.fina.1_ (contextual in the ligature).
  - Move _hh.medi.1_ to _hX.medi.0_.
  - Treat _lvs_ as a vowel.
  - Remove `post_bowed` for _a_ (now via FVS).
- For Manchu Ali Gali,
  - Rename the written unit _Al_ to _Aw_ and _Ds_ to _Dw_.
  - Use _Hx2_ (of _k_) for _Hx.fina_.
  - Use _M3_ for _M.fina_.
  - Remove the unattested _Jq.fina_, _Jh.fina_, _Cc.fina_, _Sc.fina_, _St.fina_, _St2.fina_ and _Sx.fina_.
  - Add _Dt.init_.
  - Remove the written unit _Pb2_ as an erroneous unit.
  - Renumber _i.medi.2_ as _i.medi.1_ and add _i.medi.2_; renumber _i.fina.2_ as _i.fina.1_ and _i.fina.3_ as _i.fina.2_; remove _i.fina.1_ and _i.fina.4_.
  - Assign FVS2 to medial _Hy_ of _ghX_.
  - Swap _t.isol.0_ with _t.isol.1_, _t.init.1_ with _t.init.2_, and _t.medi.1_ with _t.medi.3_.
  - Add _Nx_ for _n_ and _Wd_, _Wc_ for _bhX_ (Transitional Manchu).
  - Remove `devsger` for _s_ (now via FVS).
